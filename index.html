About

Quiet Company is a meditation timer with a focal point. Choose a scene and set your length; a chime opens and closes each sit, so you never have to check the time. With your eyes closed, let the sound carry you. With them open, rest your gaze on the scene, and when someone is breathing with you, match your breath to theirs.

Quiet Company

Quiet Company is a meditation timer with a focal point.

Choose a scene and set your length. If you meditate with your eyes closed, let the sound carry you. If you keep them open, rest your gaze on the scene. When someone is breathing with you, match your breath to theirs. Something steady to return to whenever the mind wanders.
